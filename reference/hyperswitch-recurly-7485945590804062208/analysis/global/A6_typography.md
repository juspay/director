### **Task A6: Typography & Layout System Analysis**

This document provides a detailed specification for the typography and layout system used in the video, designed for a designer to reproduce the visual elements.

---

#### **1. Capability & UI Tiles**

This style applies to the smaller, informational tiles seen throughout the video.

*   **Examples:** "Renewal Success", "Secure Payments", "Subscriptions", "Retries", "APMs", "Revenue Analytics".
*   **On-screen:** From 00:00 to 00:08.

**Typography:**
*   **Typeface:** A geometric sans-serif, very similar to **Circular Std** or **Plus Jakarta Sans**. Key identifiers are the single-story 'a', circular 'o', and the clean, modern letterforms.
*   **Weight:** Book or Medium (approx. 400-500).
*   **Case:** Title Case (e.g., "Secure Payments").
*   **Tracking:** Slightly open, estimated at +10 to +25.
*   **Relative Size:** The icon's height is approximately 1.5x the x-height of the text.

**Layout:**
*   **Tile:** A white, rounded rectangle with a soft, diffused drop shadow, giving it a floating, "keycap" appearance.
    *   **Background Color:** White (approx. `#FFFFFF`).
    *   **Corner Radius:** High, approximately 25-30% of the tile's height.
    *   **Shadow:** Soft, gray, offset down and to the right, creating a sense of depth.
*   **Content Alignment:** Vertically stacked and centered within the tile.
*   **Icon:**
    *   **Position:** Centered horizontally, placed above the text label.
    *   **Color:** A consistent indigo/blue (approx. `#3B49E0`).
    *   **Padding:** The space between the bottom of the icon and the top of the text is roughly equal to the x-height of the text.
*   **Text Label:**
    *   **Position:** Centered horizontally, placed below the icon.
    *   **Color:** The same indigo/blue as the icon (approx. `#3B49E0`).
    *   **Padding:** Generous padding on all sides. The distance from the text to the left/right edges is approximately 2x the width of the icon.

*   **"99.999%" Value:**
    *   **Weight:** Medium or Bold (approx. 500-700).

#### **4. Recurly Brand Tile**

*   **On-screen:** From 00:04 to 00:14.

**Typography (Wordmark):**
*   **Typeface:** Custom brand wordmark for **Recurly**. It is a geometric sans-serif with distinctive features, particularly the custom 'R' which is integrated into the logo mark, and the curved terminals on the 'r' and 'l'.
*   **Weight:** Appears Bold (approx. 700).
*   **Case:** Title Case ("Recurly").
*   **Color:** Black (approx. `#000000`).

**Layout (Lockup & Tile):**
*   **Tile:** A large, vibrant yellow, rounded rectangle.
    *   **Background Color:** Yellow (approx. `#FFD400`).
    *   **Corner Radius:** High, consistent with other tiles (approx. 20-25% of height).
    *   **Shadow:** Soft, diffused drop shadow.
*   **Lockup:** The logo consists of a custom mark and the wordmark.
    *   **Mark:** A stylized, lowercase 'r' formed from a continuous, looping line. It is placed to the left of the wordmark.
    *   **Wordmark:** "Recurly".
    *   **Arrangement:** The mark and wordmark are horizontally aligned and centered as a single unit within the yellow tile. The baseline of the wordmark aligns with the bottom of the mark.
    *   **Padding:** Generous, equal padding on all four sides of the centered lockup.
