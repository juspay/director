### TASK A4 — CAMERA & MOTION DESIGN

**1. Camera Path**

The camera follows a single, continuous, smoothly animated path throughout the video.

*   **0.0s - 2.0s:** The camera starts in a close-up and performs a slow **dolly out** while simultaneously **trucking right**.
*   **2.0s - 4.5s:** The camera continues to **truck right** and slightly **dolly up** (away from the surface), while also **tilting down** to keep the grid of tiles centered. This combined move reveals more of the 3D environment to the right and below the initial frame.
*   **4.5s - 6.5s:** The camera motion shifts to a **dolly in** towards the center of the grid, focusing on the rising "Recurly" tile.
*   **6.5s - 9.0s:** The camera reverses direction into a significant **dolly out**, pulling back to reveal the final wide composition of the two brand logos.
*   **9.0s - 14.5s:** The camera holds this final wide framing, executing an extremely subtle, almost imperceptible slow drift for the remainder of the shot.

**2. 3D Space**

The scene is rendered in a 3D perspective space, not an isometric one. The camera is positioned above the grid of tiles, looking down at an approximate angle of 30-40 degrees. There is significant parallax, with foreground tiles moving more quickly across the frame than background tiles during camera trucks, which creates a strong sense of depth and separation between the layers of floating elements.

**3. Element Motion**

*   **Floating:** All tiles exhibit a gentle, continuous, and subtle floating or bobbing motion, moving slightly up and down and side-to-side, as if suspended in a calm liquid.
*   **Settling/Rising:** Key tiles ("Subscriptions," "Retries," "APMs," "Recurly," "Juspay hyperswitch," and "Live Now") animate into place by rising up from below the main plane. This motion is animated with a very smooth **ease-out** curve, where they start moving quickly and then decelerate gracefully as they reach their final position. There is no overshoot, spring, or bounce; the motion is controlled and fluid.
*   **"Pressing":** The tiles do not "press" down like keys on a keyboard; their primary animation is rising into view and the subsequent gentle float.

**4. Depth of Field**

The depth of field is extremely shallow throughout the entire video, creating a soft, dreamlike aesthetic.

*   At **0.0s**, the focal plane is precisely on the "Secure Payments" tile. The "Renewal Success" tile just above it is already softly out of focus.
*   From **0.0s to 4.5s**, the focal plane travels with the camera, keeping the tiles in the center of the frame sharp while the foreground and background elements are heavily blurred.
*   From **4.5s to 9.0s**, the focus is actively "racked" or pulled to follow the main hero elements as they rise into view: first to the "Recurly" tile, then holding focus on the central area as the "Juspay hyperswitch" and "Live Now" tiles appear.
*   From **9.0s to 14.5s**, the focus is locked on the final composition: the "Recurly" and "Juspay hyperswitch" logos, with the "Live Now" button between them. All surrounding tiles are rendered in soft focus.

**5. Timing/Rhythm**

The motion is tightly synchronized to the ambient, rhythmic electronic music track.

*   **2.0s:** A musical swell coincides with the camera revealing the "Subscriptions," "Retries," and "APMs" tiles rising into view.
*   **4.5s:** A distinct musical beat and textural shift marks the moment the "Recurly" tile rises prominently into the focal plane.
*   **6.5s:** Another beat in the music cues the rise of the "Juspay hyperswitch" tile.
*   **8.5s:** A final musical swell and resolving chord aligns with the "Live Now" button appearing and the camera pulling back to settle on the final lock-up.

**6. Reproduction Note**

To recreate this, one would use a 3D animation suite (e.g., Cinema 4D, Blender). The setup would consist of a single virtual camera animated along a smooth spline path to achieve the continuous dolly/truck/tilt moves. The camera's depth of field would be enabled with a very wide aperture (low f-stop), and its focal distance would be animated to keyframe points on the hero tiles at specific times. The tiles' vertical (Z-axis) motion would be animated with smooth, non-linear easing (e.g., a cubic or sine ease-in/out curve). A subtle, low-frequency noise or wiggle expression would be applied to the tiles' X, Y, and Z positions to create the constant, gentle floating motion. All animation keyframes would be timed precisely to the beats and swells of the music track.
